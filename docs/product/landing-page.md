# Space Zero — Landing Page

## Goal

The landing page should explain Space Zero within seconds and move the visitor toward trying the product.

It should communicate one core idea:

**Space Zero does the travel work for you.**

## Section 1 — Hero

### Purpose

Immediately explain what Space Zero is and why it is different.

### Content

* Strong headline
* Short explanation
* Primary CTA
* Product visual showing the agent handling a trip

### Primary CTA

**Plan a trip**

---

## Section 2 — The Problem

Show the fragmented nature of travel.

```text
Search → Compare → Book → Pay → Monitor → Rebook
```

The user currently coordinates all of it.

Space Zero coordinates it for them.

---

## Section 3 — How It Works

Three steps:

### 01 — Tell Space Zero

Describe your trip, budget, preferences, and authority.

### 02 — Give it room to act

Space Zero plans and executes the journey within your rules.

### 03 — Let it handle the unexpected

When something changes, Space Zero finds a solution and acts within your limits.

---

## Section 4 — Autonomous Recovery

This is the hero product moment.

Show a disruption:

```text
Flight delayed
       ↓
Connection affected
       ↓
Alternative found
       ↓
Within user's authority
       ↓
Rebooked
```

The user receives the result rather than managing the crisis.

---

## Section 5 — Control

Explain that autonomy does not mean giving up control.

Show:

* Spending limits
* Automatic action permissions
* Recovery allowance
* Human approval when necessary

---

## Section 6 — Final CTA

Reinforce the product promise.

**Plan your trip. Let Space Zero handle the rest.**

CTA:

**Plan a trip**
