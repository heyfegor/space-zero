## How It Works

### Heading

**Give it the trip. Keep the control.**

### 01 — Tell it what you need

Set your destination, dates, budget, preferences, and arrival requirements.

### 02 — Let it coordinate

Space Zero searches, compares, books, and manages the journey across the services it needs.

### 03 — It handles what changes

When a disruption happens, Space Zero finds the best available solution and acts within the authority you gave it.
