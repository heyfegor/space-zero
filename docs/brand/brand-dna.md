# Space Zero — Brand DNA

## 1. Brand

**Name:** Space Zero

**Category:** Autonomous AI travel agent

**Core idea:** Give the agent the trip. Keep the decisions that matter.

**Positioning:** Space Zero handles the repetitive coordination and execution involved in travel so the traveler does not have to.

---

## 2. Brand Thesis

**The traveler should not have to be the integration layer. The agent should be.**

Space Zero connects travel services, makes routine decisions within defined boundaries, and acts when something changes.

---

## 3. Audience

Time-conscious travelers who:

* Have complex journeys
* Travel across multiple services
* Have clear budgets and preferences
* Want convenience without losing control
* Are comfortable delegating routine decisions to an agent

---

## 4. Brand Promise

**Your trip runs itself.**

Space Zero handles the work between planning and arrival.

---

## 5. Personality

Space Zero should feel:

* Calm
* Intelligent
* Capable
* Precise
* Trustworthy
* Autonomous
* Modern

It should **not** feel:

* Loud
* Gimmicky
* Overly futuristic
* Corporate
* Childish
* Crypto-native
* Like a chatbot pretending to be an agent

---

## 6. Voice

Use short, confident, factual language.

Prefer:

> Flight delayed. Connection no longer works. I found an alternative within your recovery allowance.

Avoid:

> Don't worry! I've got you covered on your exciting travel adventure.

The agent should communicate like a capable operator, not a virtual assistant trying to sound friendly.

---

## 7. Visual Direction

The visual system should communicate **control, movement, intelligence, and calm**.

Avoid generic AI aesthetics.

### Do not use

* Purple AI gradients
* Neon cyberpunk
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Generic rounded-card dashboards
* Robot imagery
* Excessive travel clichés
* Airplane emoji
* Stock-photo-heavy interfaces
* Gradient text

### Aim for

* Strong typography
* Clear hierarchy
* Generous negative space
* Precise information design
* Editorial travel references
* Subtle motion
* Distinctive but restrained visual identity
* Mobile-first layouts

---

## 8. Creative Territory

The central visual metaphor is **orchestration**.

Many disconnected travel services become one coordinated journey.

```text
Flights
Transport
Hotels
Payments
Delays
Bookings
       ↓
  SPACE ZERO
       ↓
One coordinated journey
```

The product should visually communicate that complexity is being absorbed by the agent.

---

## 9. Product-to-Brand Rule

The interface should make the agent's work visible without making the user manage it.

Show:

* What the agent is doing
* What it decided
* Why it acted
* What changed
* What the user needs to decide

Hide unnecessary operational complexity.

---

## 10. Tagline

**Your trip runs itself.**

Supporting idea:

**Plan less. Coordinate nothing.**
